When the step ahead feels too big, NeuroDesk helps you find one you can take.

Neurodivergent-friendly companion

From overwhelm to the next small step

A practical, gentle tool for neurodivergent adults — ADHD, autism, AuDHD, ODD, SLD.

How it works

In three steps, from stuck to done

Write down the task that's blocking you, even if you can't explain it well. NeuroDesk finds the first thing to act on, together with you.

1

You say what you can't do right now

Write however it comes, no need to explain it well. Choose an area: overwhelm, study, paperwork, work, daily life.

2

You get a single step

A two-minute micro-action, not a whole plan. If it's still too much, we break it into a smaller step.

3

Pick up where you left off when you come back

NeuroDesk remembers what you were working on, and you can download your steps to keep them with you.

What NeuroDesk is

Help that makes starting possible

Write down the task that's blocking you. NeuroDesk replies with a single concrete step — the one you can take right now.

One step at a time

No long lists to get through: a single two-minute micro-action, then the next one when you're ready.

Tasks that feel enormous

Study, paperwork, work, home: the tasks where starting is the hardest part become a step you can take.

No judgement, no name

You enter with a code and write however it comes. Conversations are encrypted on our server and you can delete them whenever you want.

How it works

Simple for the person, clear for whoever manages it

The person gets concrete help. Whoever supports them — a school, organisation or company — sees how it went.

For the person

Write, and get a step

Write down the task you can't start. NeuroDesk replies with a single concrete micro-action, in a tone that doesn't judge.

For the school

Issue access, not records

The school or organisation issues anonymous access codes: no name, no email. A private label is only there to remember who received it, and access can be revoked at any time.
