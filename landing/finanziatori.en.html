Gentle, inclusive technology

We help neurodivergent adults take the first step

NeuroDesk turns overwhelm into a possible step: a practical tool for people who, every day, find it hard to start. We're in a pilot phase, with a working platform, and we're looking for partners to bring it to more people.

Let's talk See the product ↓
The problem

Many neurodivergent people face everyday tasks that become an obstacle: paperwork, study, work, running a home. Often it's not the will that's missing, but help built for how their mind actually works.

The product

A companion that gives one step at a time

The person writes the task they can't start. NeuroDesk replies with a concrete micro-action, in a tone that doesn't judge.

A real Companion screen, in the pilot phase: from overwhelm to a two-minute step.

Who it's for

To the person, and to those who support them every day

The same tool, three contexts of use: the person using it stays at the centre; whoever provides it only manages the access codes.

Neurodivergent people

Adults with ADHD, autism, AuDHD, ODD or SLD who want concrete help with everyday tasks.

Schools and organisations

Schools, services and non-profit organisations supporting several people who want a practical tool to add to their work.

Companies and organisations

NeuroDesk as a staff benefit: everyday support for people who struggle to start, organise or finish a task. The company manages the access codes; conversations stay private.

What makes it different

Respect has been part of NeuroDesk from the start

Low cognitive load

A few words at a time, no long texts, clear buttons. Simplicity is part of the help.

No empty promises

NeuroDesk is a tool, not therapy, and it doesn't diagnose. It doesn't replace doctors or services.

Built with the people who use it

Features come from listening to neurodivergent people — ADHD, autism, AuDHD, ODD, SLD — and are refined with their feedback.

Where we are

A working platform, already in testers' hands

The platform, the Companion and the feedback collection already work. We're looking for partners to bring it to more people.

14 testers
Access codes handed to 14 people, to learn what to improve before opening it to everyone. (as of 28 July 2026)
5 areas
Overwhelm, study, paperwork, work, daily life: where starting is hardest.
Privacy included
Encrypted, name-free data, stored on European servers. The reply is processed by the AI provider, with the safeguards required by the GDPR.
A thank-you

NeuroDesk also grew from listening to people who talk about neurodivergence without oversimplifying it or turning it into a textbook definition.

A special thank-you to Luca Donato, whose direct, concrete and deeply human way of talking about ADHD, autism and AuDHD is a constant source of inspiration for us.
