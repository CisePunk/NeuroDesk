About us

Born from the need for a possible step

NeuroDesk is a practical, gentle tool for neurodivergent adults — ADHD, autism, AuDHD, ODD, SLD. We help turn overwhelm into the next small, concrete step — no endless lists, no judgement.

Why we do it

Starting is the hardest part

For many neurodivergent people the problem isn't the will to act, but the wall in front of the first step. NeuroDesk listens to what's blocking you and replies with one step at a time, tuned to your energy and load in the moment. It's a tool, not therapy: it doesn't replace doctors, psychologists or services.

How we work

Our principles

One step at a time

No crushing to-do lists. Just the next possible move, right now.

Kindness, not judgement

We believe you. The tone is concrete and human, never blaming.

Your data stays yours

You enter without a name; conversations are encrypted on our server and can be deleted whenever you want.

Designed with the people who use it

We build by listening to real neurodivergent people, in a pilot phase.

The team

The people behind NeuroDesk

NeuroDesk is an independent project conceived and led by Cinzia Ciprì, born from listening directly to neurodivergent people and from the wish to offer a practical, gentle tool, built together with the people who use it.
