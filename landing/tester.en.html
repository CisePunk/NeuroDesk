What to know

A few things, clearly

No need to be an expert

There's no right or wrong way to use it. We just want to understand what it's like for you.

Your data stays yours

You enter without a name. Conversations are encrypted on our server and you can delete them whenever you want.

It's not therapy

NeuroDesk is a practical tool. It doesn't replace doctors, psychologists or services.
